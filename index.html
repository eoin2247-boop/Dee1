Questions couples ask

Can’t see yours? Email celebratewithdee@gmail.com and I’ll get back to you.

Can you legally marry us?

Yes. I’m a registered solemniser, so your ceremony with me can also be your legal marriage. There’s no need for a separate civil ceremony.

What do we need to do to marry legally in Ireland?

You’ll need to give at least three months’ notice, in person, to a civil registrar. Once that’s done, you’ll receive a Marriage Registration Form (MRF), which you give to me before the ceremony.

On the day, you, your two witnesses and I sign it, and it’s returned to a registrar within a month to register your marriage. I’ll talk you through every step.

How far in advance should we book?

As soon as you have a date and venue in mind. Summer weekends book up early, so it’s worth getting in touch sooner rather than later.

Do you travel?

I’m based in the West of Ireland and happy to travel to your venue. Just tell me where you’re getting married when you get in touch.

Can we write our own vows?

Of course, and I’m happy to help. You can write them together, keep them a surprise, or repeat gentle words after me. We’ll find what feels most like you.

Can we include family, readings or traditions?

Absolutely. Readings from friends, a moment to remember loved ones, handfasting, ring warming: your ceremony can include whatever matters most to you.
